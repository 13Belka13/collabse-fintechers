? Terms
Mandate
The limits a person sets for the agent: per deal, per month, per task, when a person must confirm. Versioned and hashed.
Escrow
An on-chain safe (AuthCaptureEscrow): the buyer's money is locked; only the bank's code rules pay it out or return it.
tCZK
The bank's tokenized deposit in Czech crowns (125,00 Kč = 5 € at the demo rate); its account is published, so no §109 VAT risk.
EURC
A euro stablecoin. Paying a Czech VAT payer in EURC carries the §109 VAT liability risk a person acknowledges once.
Rule A / B / C
A: no quality claims. B: the buyer may take up to 10 % back without investigation during the claim window. C: B plus a dispute with an outside decider.
Predicates
The task's conditions as code (e.g. rating above 4.0, active in ARES). Every delivered item is checked against them; no model decides.
Client / compliance view
The client sees public reasons only; anti-money-laundering reasons stay with compliance (no tipping-off).
